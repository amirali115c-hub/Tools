Frequently Asked Questions

What is a redirect chain?

A redirect chain is a sequence of URLs where URL A redirects to URL B, which redirects to URL C, and so on until the final destination. Long chains waste crawl budget and slow down page loads.

Why should I fix redirect chains?

Redirect chains waste crawl budget, slow down page response times, and can dilute PageRank and link equity. Search engines recommend keeping chains to one redirect (ideally none).

How does the Redirect Chain Checker work?

Paste a starting URL or curl headers. The tool reconstructs and visualizes every hop in the chain, flags loops, long chains, and dead ends, and shows the final destination and HTTP status codes.

Is my data uploaded to a server?

No. All chain analysis runs locally in your browser. You can input URLs or paste curl headers manually, and the analysis never leaves your device.

Can I analyze multiple redirect chains at once?

Yes, the tool has a bulk analyzer that lets you paste many URLs or curl blocks and review all chains with an audit summary, then save or export your findings.
