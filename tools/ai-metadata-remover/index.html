Follow these simple steps to use the ai image metadata remover tool:

  2. Inspect metadata - The tool reads all embedded tags and shows a breakdown of EXIF, XMP, ICC, and AI-generated labels found.

Why Choose This Tool?

When you generate images with AI tools, the files often carry hidden metadata that identifies the generator, model, and prompt settings. Posting them publicly leaks that data. Our AI Image Metadata Remover runs entirely in your browser, so your images are scrubbed on your device and never uploaded anywhere.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is AI image metadata?

AI-generated images contain hidden EXIF and XMP tags that record the tool, model version, and generation settings. Our metadata remover strips these tags so the image carries no identifying information.

Does this tool remove EXIF data?

Yes, it removes EXIF, XMP, ICC, and IPTC metadata from JPG, PNG, and WebP images. You can preview every tag found before cleaning.

Is my image uploaded to any server?

No. All processing happens locally in your browser using client-side JavaScript. Your images never leave your device.

Can I clean multiple images at once?

Yes, batch mode lets you drop multiple files, clean them all, and download the results as individual files or a ZIP archive.

Will cleaning metadata reduce image quality?

No, removing metadata only deletes embedded data tags. The pixels and visual quality of the image are untouched.

What tags does it remove?

It strips GPS coordinates, camera settings, timestamps, software info, and AI-generation tags including prompt and model metadata.
