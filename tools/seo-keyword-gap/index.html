Frequently Asked Questions

What is keyword gap analysis?

Keyword gap analysis compares the keywords your competitors rank for against the keywords you target. The gaps are opportunities - keywords your competitors have captured that you have not yet optimized for.

How do I use the keyword gap tool?

Add your page inventory and paste competitor keyword lists or CSV exports. The tool finds missing keywords, groups them into topic clusters, scores each page's targeting, and suggests a placement plan with internal links.

Is my keyword data uploaded to a server?

No. All analysis - TF-IDF scoring, topic clustering, gap detection, and placement planning - runs entirely in your browser. Your keyword data never leaves your device.

Does this tool support CSV imports?

Yes, you can paste or upload CSV exports of your keywords and competitor keywords. The parser auto-detects columns for keyword, volume, difficulty, and ranking data.

What does the targeting planner output?

The planner recommends which keyword goes to which page, maps search intent and E-E-A-T signals, builds topic clusters, and suggests internal linking opportunities to strengthen your topical authority.
