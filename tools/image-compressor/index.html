Frequently Asked Questions

How do I compress images for free?

Use our free Image Compressor tool. Drag and drop your images, adjust quality settings or set target file size, and click Compress. The compressed images will download instantly. No uploads, no sign-ups required.

Is my image data uploaded to any server?

No, your images never leave your device. All processing happens locally in your browser using client-side JavaScript and canvas. Your privacy is 100% guaranteed.

What image formats can be compressed?

Our Image Compressor supports JPEG, PNG, WebP, and GIF. You can compress to the same format or convert to WebP for maximum savings.

How much can I compress an image?

Compression results depend on the original image. With quality set to 80%, you can typically achieve 60-80% size reduction. Converting to WebP can save an additional 25-35%.

Can I compress multiple images at once?

Yes, our Image Compressor supports batch processing. Drop multiple files and they will all be compressed with the same settings.

What is target file size mode?

Target size mode lets you specify the desired file size in KB. The tool will automatically calculate the optimal quality to hit that target.

Does compression reduce image quality?

Some quality reduction is inevitable, but our compressor uses smart algorithms to minimize visible differences. At 80% quality, most people cannot tell the difference.

Can I compare before and after compressing?

Yes, our Image Compressor shows a side-by-side comparison with a slider to compare the original and compressed images.
