Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert video to GIF for free?

Use our free Video to GIF tool. Drag and drop your video, set start/end time, adjust frame rate and resolution, and click Convert. The animated GIF will download instantly. No uploads, no sign-ups required.

Is my video data uploaded to any server?

No, your videos never leave your device. All processing happens locally in your browser using client-side JavaScript, canvas, and the GIF.js library. Your privacy is 100% guaranteed.

What video formats can be converted to GIF?

Our Video to GIF converter supports MP4, WebM, MOV, AVI, and any format supported by your browser's video element.

Can I control the GIF quality?

Yes, you can control frame rate (1-30 fps), resolution, and color palette. Higher frame rate = smoother but larger GIF. Lower resolution = smaller file size.

Can I select a specific portion of the video?

Yes, use the start/end time inputs to select the exact portion of the video you want to convert to GIF. You can also use the frame strip to visually select frames.

Can I convert multiple videos to GIF?

Yes, our Video to GIF converter supports batch mode. Add multiple videos and convert them all to GIFs with the same settings.

What is the maximum GIF length?

There is no hard limit, but longer GIFs produce larger files. We recommend keeping GIFs under 10 seconds for optimal file size and quality.

Does it work on mobile devices?

Yes, our Video to GIF converter works on all modern browsers including mobile. The interface is responsive and adapts to smaller screens.
