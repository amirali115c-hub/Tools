How to Remove Watermarks Online

Removing watermarks has never been easier. Our free online watermark remover allows you to clean up images in just a few simple steps:

  1. Drop your image - Drag and drop an image file into the upload area, or click to browse.
  2. Paint over watermark - Use the brush tool to paint over the watermark area. Adjust brush size for precision.
  3. Choose method - Select Inpaint (content-aware), Blur, Solid Color, or Clone removal method.
  4. Process and download - Click "Remove Watermark" and download your cleaned image instantly.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I remove a watermark for free?

Use our free Watermark Remover tool. Upload your image, use the brush to paint over the watermark area, choose a removal method, and click Process. The cleaned image will download instantly. No uploads, no sign-ups required.

Is my image data uploaded to any server?

No, your images never leave your device. All processing happens locally in your browser using client-side JavaScript and canvas. Your privacy is 100% guaranteed.

What removal methods are available?

Our Watermark Remover offers multiple methods: Inpaint (AI-powered content-aware fill), Blur (Gaussian blur), Solid Color (fill with surrounding color), and Clone (copy from nearby area).

Can I remove watermarks from videos?

Yes, our Watermark Remover supports both images and videos. For videos, you can select the watermark area and it will be removed from all frames.

How do I select the watermark area?

Use the brush tool to paint over the watermark. You can adjust the brush size for precision. The tool highlights the area you've painted so you can see exactly what will be removed.

Does it work on text watermarks?

Yes, our tool works well on text watermarks, logos, timestamps, and other overlaid elements. The Inpaint method is particularly effective at removing these while preserving the underlying image.

What image formats are supported?

Our Watermark Remover supports PNG, JPG/JPEG, WebP, and GIF for images, plus MP4, WebM, MOV for videos.

Can I compare before and after?

Yes, our tool shows a before/after comparison view. You can see the original with the watermark and the processed version side by side.
