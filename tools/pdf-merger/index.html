How to Merge PDF Files Online

Merging PDF files has never been easier. Our free online PDF merger allows you to combine multiple PDF documents into a single file in just a few simple steps:

  1. Drop your PDF files - Drag and drop multiple PDF files into the upload area, or click to browse and select files from your device.
  2. Reorder files - Drag and drop files in the file list to arrange them in the order you want. The merged PDF will combine files in this order.
  3. Select pages - Preview all pages and select exactly which pages you want to include in the merged document.
  4. Configure settings - Choose page size, add page numbers, and adjust other output settings.
  5. Merge and download - Click the "Merge PDFs" button and download your merged PDF instantly.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I merge PDF files for free?

Use our free PDF Merger tool. Simply drag and drop your PDF files, reorder them as needed, select pages, and click Merge. The merged PDF will download instantly. No uploads, no sign-ups required.

Is my PDF data uploaded to any server?

No, your PDF files never leave your device. All processing happens locally in your browser using client-side JavaScript. Your privacy is 100% guaranteed.

Can I select specific pages to merge?

Yes, our PDF Merger allows you to preview all pages and select exactly which pages you want to include in the merged document. You can also use page ranges.

How do I reorder PDF pages?

Simply drag and drop files in the file list to reorder them. The merged PDF will combine files in the order you specify. You can also rearrange pages within each file.

Can I add page numbers to the merged PDF?

Yes, our PDF Merger has an option to add page numbers to the merged document. You can enable this in the Settings tab before merging.

What is the best free PDF merger?

Our PDF Merger is one of the best free PDF mergers available. It offers page preview, drag reorder, page selection, page numbers, and multiple page sizes - all running privately in your browser with no uploads.

How many PDF files can I merge at once?

There is no limit to the number of PDF files you can merge. You can add as many files as you need. However, for best performance, we recommend merging files in batches of 10-20 at a time.

What page sizes are supported?

Our PDF Merger supports A4, Letter, Legal, A3, and A5 page sizes. You can select the output page size in the Settings tab before merging.
