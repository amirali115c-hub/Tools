How to Extract Audio from Video

Follow these simple steps to use the free online video to audio extractor tool:

  1. Drop your video - Drag and drop an MP4, WebM, or other video file into the extractor.
  2. Choose the output format - Pick WAV, WebM, or OGG for the extracted audio track.
  3. Trim or fade (optional) - Set start/end times or apply fades to the extracted audio.
  4. Extract and download - Convert and download the audio file instantly, or process a whole batch.

Features

  • Extract audio from MP4, WebM, and other video files
  • Output to WAV, WebM, or OGG
  • Trim to a specific segment and apply fades
  • Batch mode for multiple videos
  • 100% in-browser using the Web Audio API

Why Choose This Tool?

Pulling the audio track out of a video — a soundtrack, a lecture, a voiceover — takes seconds with our extractor. Everything runs locally with the Web Audio API, so your video never uploads to a server, and you can trim and batch-process as much as you like.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I extract audio from video?

Drop your video file, choose WAV, WebM, or OGG, and click Extract. The audio downloads instantly.

What video formats are supported?

The extractor works with common browser-supported formats including MP4 and WebM.

Is my video uploaded to a server?

No. Audio extraction happens entirely in your browser with the Web Audio API. Your files never leave your device.

Can I trim the audio during extraction?

Yes, you can set start and end times to extract only a specific segment, and apply fade in/out.

Can I process multiple videos at once?

Yes, batch mode converts several videos in one pass and downloads each result.

What output formats are available?

You can export the audio as WAV, WebM, or OGG depending on your needs.
