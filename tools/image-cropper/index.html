How to Crop Images Online

Cropping images has never been easier. Our free online image cropper allows you to crop, rotate, flip, and transform images in just a few simple steps:

  1. Drop your image - Drag and drop an image file into the upload area, or click to browse.
  2. Adjust the crop box - Drag the handles to resize the crop area. Use aspect ratio presets for common ratios.
  3. Transform as needed - Rotate 90°/180°, flip horizontal/vertical, or enable round crop for circular crops.
  4. Set output size - Enter exact dimensions or use Quick Size presets like 400x400, 1080p, IG Post.
  5. Crop and download - Click "Crop & Download" to get your cropped image instantly.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I crop an image for free?

Use our free Image Cropper tool. Drag and drop your image, adjust the crop box by dragging handles, select an aspect ratio preset, and click Crop & Download. No uploads, no sign-ups required.

Is my image data uploaded to any server?

No, your images never leave your device. All processing happens locally in your browser using client-side JavaScript and canvas. Your privacy is 100% guaranteed.

What aspect ratios are available?

Our Image Cropper supports Free (custom), 1:1 (square), 4:3, 16:9, 9:16 (portrait), and 3:4 aspect ratios. You can also set custom output dimensions.

Can I rotate and flip images?

Yes, our cropper includes Rotate Left (90°), Rotate Right (90°), Rotate 180°, Flip Horizontal, and Flip Vertical options in the Transform tab.

What is round crop?

Round crop creates a circular crop of your image, perfect for profile pictures and avatars. Enable it in the Transform tab and the crop box will become circular.

Can I set a specific output size?

Yes, enter exact pixel dimensions in the Output fields (W and H). You can also use Quick Size presets for common sizes like 400x400, 1080p, IG Post, FB Cover, and more.

What output formats are supported?

Our Image Cropper supports PNG, JPEG, and WebP output formats. You can select the format and quality in the Transform tab.

Can I undo crop changes?

Yes, our cropper includes Undo and Redo buttons. Each crop, resize, rotate, or flip action is saved to an undo stack (up to 30 states).
