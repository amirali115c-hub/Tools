Viewing CSV files has never been easier. Our free online CSV viewer allows you to analyze and explore data in just a few simple steps:

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I view CSV files for free?

Use our free CSV Viewer tool. Drag and drop your CSV file or click to browse. The data will be displayed in an interactive table with sorting, filtering, and search. No uploads, no sign-ups required.

Is my CSV data uploaded to any server?

No, your CSV files never leave your device. All processing happens locally in your browser using client-side JavaScript. Your privacy is 100% guaranteed.

Can I sort CSV data?

Yes, click any column header to sort by that column. Click again to reverse the sort order. The arrow indicator shows current sort direction.

Can I filter CSV data?

Yes, our CSV Viewer includes column-based filtering. Select a column, choose a filter condition (equals, contains, greater than, etc.), and enter a value to filter.

Can I search within the CSV?

Yes, the search box filters rows in real-time as you type. It searches across all columns simultaneously.

Can I export the filtered data?

Yes, you can export filtered/sorted data as CSV, JSON, or copy to clipboard. Only the visible (filtered) rows are exported.

What CSV file sizes are supported?

Our CSV Viewer can handle files with hundreds of thousands of rows. For very large files (1M+ rows), performance may vary depending on your device.

Does it handle different CSV delimiters?

Yes, our viewer auto-detects common delimiters including comma, semicolon, tab, and pipe. You can also manually set the delimiter if needed.
