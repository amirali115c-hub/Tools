How to Trim a Video Online

Follow these simple steps to use the free online video trimmer tool:

  1. Drop your video - Drag and drop an MP4, WebM, or MOV file into the trimmer.
  2. Set trim points - Use the frame preview and time controls to mark your start and end points.
  3. Preview the segment - Play back the selected region to confirm the cut is exactly where you want it.
  4. Export and download - Trim the video and download the clipped segment, or process a batch of files.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I trim a video?

Drop your video, set the start and end points, preview the segment, then export the trimmed clip.

What video formats are supported?

The trimmer works with browser-supported formats including MP4, WebM, and MOV.

Is my video uploaded to a server?

No. All trimming is done locally in your browser using MediaRecorder and canvas. Your video stays on your device.

Can I make precise cuts?

Yes, the frame preview lets you scrub to exact frames so your cuts are pixel-perfect.

Can I trim multiple videos at once?

Yes, batch mode lets you trim several videos with the same settings in one pass.

Can I create multiple segments?

Yes, you can cut a video into multiple segments and export them separately.
