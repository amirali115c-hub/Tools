How to Trim Audio Online

Trimming audio has never been easier. Our free online audio trimmer allows you to cut audio files in just a few simple steps:

  1. Drop your audio - Drag and drop an audio file into the upload area, or click to browse.
  2. Set trim points - Drag the handles on the waveform or type exact start/end times.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I trim audio for free?

Use our free Audio Trimmer tool. Drag and drop your audio file, drag the trim handles to select the portion you want, and click Trim. The trimmed audio will download instantly. No uploads, no sign-ups required.

Is my audio data uploaded to any server?

No, your audio files never leave your device. All processing happens locally in your browser using client-side JavaScript and Web Audio API. Your privacy is 100% guaranteed.

What audio formats are supported?

Our Audio Trimmer supports MP3, WAV, OGG, M4A, FLAC, and AAC. You can trim any of these formats and download in the same format.

Can I trim multiple audio files at once?

Yes, our Audio Trimmer supports batch mode. Add multiple files and trim them all with the same start/end points.

Can I add fade in/out effects?

Yes, our trimmer includes fade in and fade out options. Set the duration and the tool will apply smooth volume transitions at the start and end.

Can I see the waveform?

Yes, our Audio Trimmer displays a visual waveform of your audio file. You can drag trim handles directly on the waveform for precise selection.

How precise is the trimming?

Our trimmer offers millisecond precision. You can type exact start/end times or drag the handles on the waveform for visual precision.

Can I preview before trimming?

Yes, you can play the selected portion before trimming. The preview plays only the area between the trim handles so you hear exactly what will be output.
