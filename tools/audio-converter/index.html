How to Convert Audio Files Online

Follow these simple steps to use the free online audio converter tool:

  1. Drop your audio files - Drag and drop MP3, WAV, WebM, or OGG files into the converter, or click to browse.
  2. Pick the output format - Choose WAV, WebM, OGG, or MP3 and set the sample rate and channel count.
  3. Trim or fade (optional) - Cut to a start/end region or apply fade in/out before converting.
  4. Convert and download - Click Convert and download the processed files instantly, or grab a ZIP of a whole batch.

Features

  • Converts between WAV, WebM, OGG, and MP3

  • Trim to exact start/end times and apply fades
  • Batch convert many files at once

Why Choose This Tool?

Most online audio converters upload your files to a server and force you to wait, sign up, or accept watermarks. Our Audio Converter processes everything locally with the Web Audio API, so your audio never leaves your device. Convert, trim, and batch-process without limits.

Frequently Asked Questions

What audio formats can I convert?

You can convert between WAV, WebM, OGG, and MP3. The tool also lets you set sample rate and mono or stereo channels for the output.

Is my audio uploaded to any server?

No. Conversion runs entirely in your browser using the Web Audio API. Your files never leave your device.

Can I convert multiple files at once?

Yes, batch mode lets you drop many files, set one output format, and convert them all. Results can be downloaded individually or as a ZIP.

Can I trim audio before converting?

Yes, you can set start and end times to trim the clip, and apply fade in/out effects before conversion.

Does conversion reduce audio quality?

WAV output is lossless. For compressed formats, you control the sample rate, so you can balance file size and quality.

What sample rates are supported?

The converter supports 44100 Hz (CD quality), 48000 Hz (DVD/video quality), and 22050 Hz (low bandwidth).
